About Liravex

Rooted in Tamil Nadu. Built for global trade.

Liravex is an export, import and e-commerce business based in Tamil Nadu, India. We work directly with weavers and manufacturers in the region to bring heritage textiles and everyday home products to buyers who value authenticity and quality.

We serve importers, retailers and boutiques through B2B export, and reach end customers directly through online marketplaces.
